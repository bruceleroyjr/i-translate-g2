See it AND hear it. I-Translate is live two-way translation for the Even G2 that also speaks. Their words appear translated on your glasses, and your phone reads them aloud - through its speaker or straight into your Bluetooth earbuds. Most translator apps only show text; I-Translate lets you listen too.

HEAR EVERY LINE
- Their words, read to you in your language - in your earbuds, eyes on the person
- Your words, spoken in their language from the phone speaker
- Turn each side on or off with the speaker in its language pill
- Tap the speaker next to any line to hear it again

REPLY AND ASK IDEAS
Each translated line comes with one suggested reply and one question. Pick one and tap: your phone says it in their language.

YOUR TURN
Double tap, say it in your own language, and tap to send. The translation is spoken to the other person.

ON YOUR PHONE
- The whole conversation, with play buttons on every line
- History: past conversations with short titles, saved on your phone
- Glasses mics or iPhone mic, and one-tap language swap

13 LANGUAGES
English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese (Mandarin and Cantonese), Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino and Hawaiian.

YOUR KEYS, YOUR PHONE
No account and no server in between. Add your own Soniox key and a key for the AI you choose: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Mistral, Groq or OpenRouter. Keys and history stay on your phone; mics stream only while you translate.
