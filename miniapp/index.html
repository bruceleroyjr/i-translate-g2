Translation

App

I-Translate

On the glasses

Reply ideas: a reply and a question under each translation. Swipe to the one you want, tap to say it in their language (the phone plays it). Double tap to say or type your own.

Timing

The translation keeps running while the display is off; the next line lights it again. Nothing is saved: no notes, no recordings.

Voices

Translation voices

Picking a voice plays a sample. The glasses have no speaker: the phone speaks. Tap Test sound once to allow the phone to play audio.

API keys

I-Translate runs on two services you pay for directly: Soniox hears, an AI of your choice translates. Your keys stay on this phone and go only to those services.

AI that translates

Provider

Soniox: create a key at console.soniox.com (about $0.12 per hour of listening). The AI: a fast, small model costs a fraction of a cent per line; leave Model empty for the app's pick. Set a spending limit on both.

  • StartThe app opens on the setup card: tap Start for your usual pair, or tap They speak / You read to change one, or Swap. A dark display: tap to bring the card back.
